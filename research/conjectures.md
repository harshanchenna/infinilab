# Conjectures (machine-discovered, candidate)

New empirical relations/patterns the loop has found and tested to high precision
but that are NOT already-known results. Each must state: the relation, the
precision it survived to, the search that produced it, and an honest assessment
of whether it is likely already known. These are candidates for human follow-up,
not theorems. Precedent: the Ramanujan Machine surfaced genuinely new
conjectured identities this way.

Status legend: PROPOSED (survived numerics) | KNOWN (turned out to be standard)
| REFUTED (failed at higher precision) | PROMISING (survived + plausibly novel).
